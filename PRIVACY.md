# Privacy Policy for CoverCraft AI

Last updated: July 25, 2026

CoverCraft AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the CoverCraft AI Chrome extension.

## 1. Information We Collect
*   **Resume Text:** When you upload a resume, the text is extracted and stored **locally** on your device using `chrome.storage.local`.
*   **API Keys:** Your Google Gemini API Key is stored **locally** on your device using `chrome.storage.local` and is never sent to us.
*   **Job Descriptions:** Any job description text you paste is only processed during the generation request.

## 2. How We Process Your Data
*   **Local Storage:** All sensitive data (Resumes, API Keys) remains on your local machine and is not synced to Google Cloud or any of our servers.
*   **AI Processing:** When you generate a cover letter, your resume and the job description are sent securely via HTTPS to your specified Gemini API endpoint (directly to Google Gemini using your provided API key). We do not store, view, or collect your resumes or generated cover letters on our servers.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Your data is only sent to Google Gemini API for the sole purpose of generating your cover letter.
